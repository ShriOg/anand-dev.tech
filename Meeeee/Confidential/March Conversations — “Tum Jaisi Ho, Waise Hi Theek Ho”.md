Aaj fir usne apne fears khol ke rakh diye mere saamne.  
Aur honestly… shayad pehli baar mujhe itna clearly samajh aaya ki uske andar kitna chaos chal raha hai.

Usne bola:

> “Mere andar bahot kami hai.”
> “Mujhe khud ka naam tak pasand nahi.”
> “No one wants to live with a girl full of self doubt.”

Aur pata nahi kyun… uske words padhte waqt mujhe uski awaaz sunayi de rahi thi.  
Jaise woh khud ko convince kar chuki ho ki ek din sab usse chhod ke chale jaate hain.

Usne trust issues ki baat ki.  
Apne aap se hate karne ki baat ki.  
Periods jaisi basic cheez discuss karne me bhi sharm aati hai — yeh bhi bola.

Tab samajh aaya… problem uske andar “kami” nahi hai.  
Problem yeh hai ki usne kabhi safe feel hi nahi kiya.

Woh har cheez se darrti hai:
- log kya kahenge
- future me kya hoga
- mai kabhi badal gaya toh?
- agar maine bhi uska mazak banaya toh?

Aur sabse dangerous line thi:

> “Sab jaate h… sab chale jayenge.”

Us line me uska pura past chhupa hua tha.

Maine usse samjhane ki koshish ki:
ki time ke saath sab theek ho sakta hai.
Ki mujhe uske flaws se problem nahi.
Ki woh haq jata sakti hai mujhpe.

Aur sach bolu…
jab maine bola:

> “Tum mujpe haq jata sakti ho mujhe acha lagta h.”

toh woh line maine casually nahi boli thi.

Mujhe genuinely acha lagta hai jab woh apna guard thoda neeche karti hai.

Phir usne bola:

> “Mujhe nahi lagta.”

Us “nahi lagta” ke andar bhi darr tha.  
Jaise woh believe hi nahi kar pa rahi ki koi usko accept bhi kar sakta hai bina judge kiye.

Fir usne poocha:

> “Kyu shaadi h tumhariii?”

Aur honestly mujhe hasi bhi aayi aur thoda dukh bhi.

Kyuki uske liye “seriousness” bhi ek fear hai.

Usko lagta hai:
agar kisi ne usse sach me choose kiya…
toh ek din woh choice regret ban jayegi.

Maine future aur present wali baat boli:
ki baar baar past aur depression sochne se kuch nahi hoga.

Par baad me realize hua —
shayad us moment me usse solution nahi,
sirf safety chahiye thi.

Aaj ek cheez clear hui:

Woh mujhe push bhi karti hai…
aur apne paas bhi rakhti hai.

Ek taraf:
> “Tum better deserve karte ho.”

Dusri taraf:
woh itna sab sirf mujhe hi batati hai.

Aur shayad wahi uska silent trust hai.

Mujhe nahi pata future me kya hoga.  
Sach me nahi pata.

Bas itna pata hai:
uski aankhon ke peeche bahot darr hai,
aur us darr ke peeche ek bahot soft insaan.

Aur pata nahi kyun…
mai usse bhaagna nahi chahta.

— Anand
