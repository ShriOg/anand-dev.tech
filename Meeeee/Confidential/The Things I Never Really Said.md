## Abhilasha

Pata nhi ye note kab dubara kholunga. Shayad kabhi bhi nahi. Shayad kisi random raat ko jab chest fir heavy lagegi aur neend nahi aayegi. But jo bhi hai, mujhe ye save karna tha because mai rarely open up karta hu.

Sach bolu toh mai thak gaya hu. Bahut zyada. Sirf relationship se nahi, khud se bhi. And the worst part is ki mujhe exact moment bhi yaad nahi jab sab tootna start hua. Bas ek din realize hua ki mai andar se empty sa ho gaya hu.

Mujhe ab bhi park yaad h. Mai aaj bhi kabhi kabhi bas tumhe dekhne chala jata hu. Door se. Bina baat kiye. Bina disturb kiye. Aur ajeeb baat ye h ki usme bhi thodi si happiness mil jaati h. Jaise bas itna enough ho ki tum exist karti ho.

Maine bahut try kiya strong banne ka but honestly nahi ho paya mujhse. Relationship tootne ke baad sab pehle jaisa nahi ban pata. Bahut delicate ho jata h sab. Ek wrong word aur sab fir bikhar jata h. Shayad mujhse sambhal hi nahi paya.

Mujhe ab bhi yaad h last year June-July ki hamari baatein. Kitni peaceful hua karti thi. Us time mujhe genuinely lagta tha ki maybe life itni buri nahi hoti. Tumhare aane ke baad hi mai thoda logo se baat karne laga tha. Mukesh se bhi properly tumhare baad hi friendship type start hui. Wrna mai months tak kisi se baat nahi karta tha.

Aur fir 16 April.

Tum so gayi thi. Mai poori raat jag raha tha. Bohot kuch chal raha tha dimaag me. Overthinking, fear, guilt, attachment, loneliness sab ek saath. Aur us raat maine suicide attempt kiya.

Aaj bhi type karte hue weird lag raha h ye sentence.

Maine knot bana li thi. Fir kya hua exactly mujhe nahi pata. Shayad stress ya panic ki wajah se behosh ho gaya. Jab hosh aaya toh around 2 baje the. Poora body sweaty thi, naak se blood aa raha tha. Fir maine muh dhoya, paani piya, terrace pe walk kiya aur wapas aake bed pe let gaya.

Us raat maine approx 66 messages unsend kiye the tumhari chat se.

Uske baad poore hafte neend nahi aayi. Health bhi kharab ho gayi thi. Aur funny part? Kisi ko pata bhi nahi chala. Na family ko. Na friends ko. Kyunki mai waise bhi kisi ko kuch batata nahi hu.

Ab bhi love karta hu tumse. Shayad hamesha karta rahunga in some form. But ab pyar se zyada darr lagta h. Darr ki fir hurt na ho jaaye sab. Darr ki mai fir mentally wahi state me na pahoch jaun.

Mujhe nahi lagta move on jaldi hoga. Ho bhi gaya toh shayad tumhari memory kabhi fully nahi jayegi. Aur honestly ab mai force bhi nahi karta khud ko bhoolne ke liye. Kuch feelings ko bas rehne dena padta h.

Tum Navy jaana chahti thi. Mujhe ab bhi yaad h. I really hope tum achieve karo wo sab jo tum deserve karti ho. Tum settle ho jao, happy raho, safe raho. Future me tumhari life achi ho — maybe wahi enough closure h mere liye.

Aur agar kabhi future me randomly mil gaye na kahi, toh awkward mat feel karna. Bas ek smile enough hogi.

I think tumne meri life me jitna character development kiya h na, utna kisi aur cheez ne nahi kiya hota. Pehle mai emotionally itna aware bhi nahi tha. Ab hu. Chahe painful way me hi sahi.

Still… worth it tha.

Har second.
