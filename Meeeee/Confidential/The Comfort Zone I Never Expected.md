## April Nights

Kabhi kabhi life mein ek insaan achanak itna close feel hone lagta hai ki samajh hi nahi aata yeh sab kab start hua.  
Hum bas baat karte karte ek dusre ki aadat ban gaye the. Random talks, late night conversations, stupid jokes, emotional moments — sab kuch naturally flow karta tha.

Aur phir ek din usne woh line boli jo shayad mere dimaag mein bahut time tak rehne wali hai.

> “Sach batau like mujhe sachme periods par baat karne mein sharm aati hai. Mein mummy se baat nahi kar pati, tumse kar pati hu jo baatein share nahi kar pati tumse karti hu like comfort zone ho.”

Us moment pe mujhe realize hua ki trust actually kitni badi cheez hoti hai.

## Uska Trust

Periods jaisi cheez pe openly baat karna shayad kisi ke liye normal ho, but uske liye nahi tha.  
Woh apni mummy tak se woh baatein nahi kar paati thi.  
Phir bhi mere saath woh comfortable feel karti thi.

Aur honestly… woh line sirf ek sentence nahi thi.

Woh ek feeling thi.

Ek silent acceptance.  
Ek emotional safety.  
Ek jagah jahan woh pretend nahi karti thi.

## Mere Liye Kya Tha Yeh?

Mujhe nahi pata tha ki main kisi ka “comfort zone” ban sakta hu.  
Main usually apni feelings openly show nahi karta.  
Main khud itna guarded rehta hu ki logon ko andar aane hi nahi deta.

But uske saath sab alag tha.

Uski vulnerability dekh ke mujhe laga shayad kisi ne pehli baar mujhe sirf “useful” ya “funny” nahi dekha…  
balki safe dekha.

Aur yeh feeling dangerous bhi hoti hai.

Kyuki jab koi tumhare saath itna open ho jata hai na… tum unknowingly us insaan ko protect karna start kar dete ho.

## The Attachment

Uski har random baat important lagne lagi thi.  
Har notification mood affect karta tha.  
Har “goodnight” oddly peaceful lagta tha.

Aur shayad wahi point tha jahan attachment silently grow ho gayi.

Without announcement.  
Without confession.  
Without labels.

Bas ho gayi.

## The Part I Rarely Say Out Loud

Main generally cheezein save nahi karta.  
Memories ko ignore karta hu.  
Conversations delete kar deta hu.

But kuch moments hote hain jo delete nahi hote.

Yeh unme se ek hai.

Kisi ka tumhe apna comfort zone kehna…  
shayad duniya ke liye small thing ho.

Mere liye nahi tha.
