## 6 October 2025 — 10:13 PM

Aaj raat bas clarity chahiye thi.  
Na ladai.  
Na drama.  
Na explanations.

Bas ek simple sawaal.

> “Rishta rakhna h ya nhi?”

Mujhe laga answer simple hoga.  
Ya toh “haan”  
Ya “na”

Par uske “Nhi🙂” aur uske turant baad wale “Haa” ne bata diya ki baat utni simple kabhi thi hi nahi.

Us raat mujhe pehli baar feel hua ki kuch log jaate waqt bhi rukna chahte hain.

## Uske Words

- “Main galat insaan hu.”
- “Tumhari life barbaad nahi kar sakti.”
- “Mujhe itna pyaar deserve nahi hua.”
- “Find someone.”
- “Bhool jao.”

Aur sabse heavy line:

> “Main woh hu jisse marne ke khayal zyada aate h…”

Us moment pe mujhe samajh nahi aa raha tha kya zyada hurt kar raha hai —
uska jaana,
ya uska khud ko itna kam samajhna.
