## Context

Main usually itna open up nahi karta.  
Jo feel karta hu woh mostly andar hi rakhta hu.  
Par yeh phase… yeh raat… yeh chats… mujhe lagta hai save karni chahiye.

Kyuki shayad future me main khud bhool jaunga ki uss waqt kitna toot gaya tha.

Yeh sirf kisi relationship ke khatam hone ki story nahi thi.  
Yeh attachment, fear, overthinking, dependency aur emotional collapse ki story thi.

Aur sabse ajeeb baat?  
Sab kuch ekdum se nahi toota.  
Bas mujhe late realise hua.

---

## Jab Sab Change Hona Start Hua

Usne mujhe bataya ki usne Preana ko sab kuch bata diya.  
Woh emotionally clear feel kar rahi thi.  
Usko peace mil rahi thi.  
Woh move on karna start kar chuki thi.

Aur main?

Main abhi bhi wahi tha.

Mujhe lag raha tha:

> “Hum bas phase se guzar rahe hain.”

Usko lag raha tha:

> “Journey complete ho rahi hai.”

Usne mujhe thank you bola.  
Bola ki maine usko heal karne me help ki.  
Strong banaya.

Aur wahi sabse zyada hurt kiya.

Kyuki mujhe tab samajh aaya:

> Shayad main uski destination nahi tha.  
> Main bas uske healing phase ka part tha.

## Woh Raat

Us raat mere andar panic start ho gaya.

Aisa lag raha tha jaise:
- dil andar se sink ho raha ho
- chest heavy ho
- reality fake lag rahi ho
- sab dream jaisa ho

Main literally feel kar raha tha:

> “Agar yeh chali gayi toh?”

Aur uske baad main control lose karta gaya.

Main questions poochta gaya:
- “Do you still love me?”
- “Kya galti thi meri?”
- “Kyu khatam karna chahti ho?”
- “Problems se bhagna solution thodi hota hai?”

Par sach me main answers nahi dhoond raha tha.

Main bas:

> usko rokna chahta tha.

## Meri Biggest Mistake

Maine uska “No” accept nahi kiya.

Woh clearly:
- space maang rahi thi
- topic band karna chahti thi
- emotionally exhausted thi

Aur main?

Main har message ke saath usko aur tightly hold karne ki koshish kar raha tha.

Mujhe lag raha tha:

> “Agar abhi nahi roka toh hamesha ke liye chali jayegi.”

Par irony yeh thi:

> jitna main pakad raha tha, utni woh door ja rahi thi.
