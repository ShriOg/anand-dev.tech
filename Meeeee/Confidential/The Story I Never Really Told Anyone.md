## Abhilasha — The Chaos That Somehow Felt Like Peace

I don’t even know where to start honestly.  
Maybe from the fact that this was never a “normal” relationship.

No confession at first.  
No official tag.  
Bas baatein.  
Random conversations.  
Reels.  
Park walks.  
Masti.  
Food.  
Late night talks till 3–5 AM.  
Aur dheere dheere… she became part of my everyday life.

Not “part” actually.  
More like… background music of my whole existence.

---

## How It Started

She was technically my friend’s sister from school side.  
We already knew each other a little.

Then one night around April 20–21, around 12–1 AM, we talked properly for the first time.

And after that…

Everything changed slowly.

Within 1–2 months, we were talking like couples without ever confessing anything.  
We met in parks.  
Played around.  
Ate together.  
Made fun of each other.

Simple things.

But somehow those simple things became the best part of my days.

---

## The First Time I Actually Cared

Before her, I was emotionally closed.

I rarely opened up to anyone.  
Never really cared deeply for people.  
Never cried for someone.

Then she happened.

And suddenly:
- I started remembering tiny details about someone.
- Started worrying if she ate or slept.
- Started waiting for messages.
- Started smiling randomly.

And for the first time in my life…

I cried because of another person.

That scared me honestly.

Because I realized:
> “Damn… I really love this girl.”

---

## Her Past

One day she finally opened up.

And I think that conversation changed me forever.

She told me about:
- harassment incidents from childhood,
- teachers failing her,
- friends betraying her trust,
- people blaming her instead of protecting her,
- family chaos,
- domestic violence around her,
- fear,
- self-hate,
- guilt.

The worst part?

She genuinely believed:
> “Maybe I’m the problem.”

That line broke something inside me.

Because I could see it clearly:
She wasn’t bad.  
She was just hurt for too long.

---

## The Push-Pull Cycle

Our story became chaos after that.

She would:
- come close,
- confess feelings,
- then suddenly panic,
- say she’ll leave,
- block me,
- unblock me,
- apologize,
- disappear,
- come back again.

At one point we probably had 20–30 breakups and patch-ups.

Sounds toxic from outside maybe.

But weirdly…

Every time she came back, it still felt peaceful to me.

Not because things were perfect.  
But because her presence itself felt familiar.

Like home after a hard day.

---

## Her Biggest Fear

She always believed:
- she was “impure”
- she had too many flaws
- she’d ruin my life
- one day I’d judge her too

No matter how many times I told her:
> “Tumhari galti nahi thi.”

She never fully believed it.

Sometimes she’d say:
> “Mujhe koi nahi chahiye.”

But honestly, I think what she actually meant was:
> “I’m scared to need someone.”

---

## The Weirdest Thing

We weren’t even officially together half the time.

Yet somehow:
- every song reminded me of her,
- every park corner,
- every cold morning,
- every late-night silence,
- every stupid meme,
- every random child in parks saying “I love you didi”…

Everything became attached to her.

Even now, I don’t know how to explain it properly.

She didn’t just become “someone I loved.”

She became part of how my mind worked.

---

## The Nights I’ll Never Forget

Those 3 AM conversations…

Where:
- she typed random keyboard spam while sleepy,
- talked about teddy bears,
- childhood memories,
- fears,
- dreams,
- navy goals,
- football,
- crop tops she was scared to wear,
- becoming independent,
- escaping society,
- taking care of her parents someday.

And somehow between trauma and jokes…

We still laughed.

A lot.

That’s what made her special honestly.

Even after everything she went through, she still laughed like a child sometimes.

---

## The Thing Nobody Understands

People outside probably think:
> “Why stay in something so messy?”

But they don’t understand one thing.

She gave me something nobody else ever did:
> emotional honesty.

Raw, messy, confusing honesty.

No filters.

No pretending to be perfect.

Just a deeply hurt person trying to survive.

And somehow…
I loved all of it.

Even the chaos.

---

## What I Feel Now

Honestly?

I still don’t fully understand what this feeling is.

It’s deeper than attachment.  
More peaceful than obsession.  
More painful than normal love.

Maybe it’s just:
> loving someone you can’t completely save.

And accepting that anyway.

---

## If She Ever Reads This

Abhilasha,

I don’t hate you for the chaos.  
Never did.

I know you tried protecting me in your own weird way.  
Even when it hurt both of us.

And maybe one day:
- we’ll become strangers,
- maybe life will separate us,
- maybe we’ll laugh about all this years later.

But one thing will probably stay true forever:

> You were the first person who made me feel emotions this deeply.

And honestly?

That alone changed me forever.

— Anand
