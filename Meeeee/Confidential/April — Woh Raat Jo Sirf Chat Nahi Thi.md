## Her Trauma — Aur Mere Liye Sab Kuch Change Ho Jana

Conversation ka sabse heavy part woh tha jab usne apne triggers aur fears ke baare me khulna start kiya.

- kuch childhood memories trigger hoti hain
- child abuse related content dekhna oddly comforting lagta h because:
  
> “Glti aapki nahi h.”

Us line ne mujhe tod diya thoda.

Us moment pe mujhe realise hua ki woh intensity nahi dhoondh rahi.  
Woh safety dhoondh rahi hai.

Uska dream bhi notice karne layak tha:

> “Naraz ho kar husband ke paas jao aur woh tightly hug kare aur safe sleep mil jaye.”

Not drama.  
Not obsession.  
Not toxicity.

Bas safety.
