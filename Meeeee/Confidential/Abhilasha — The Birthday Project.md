## 28 March

Kabhi kabhi na… kuch log life mein itne naturally important ho jaate hain ki unke liye “Happy Birthday” type karna bhi enough nahi lagta.  
Aur shayad iss baar mere saath bhi wahi hua.

Main soch raha tha ki tujhe kaise wish karu.  
Normal text?  
Story?  
Paragraph?  
Voice note?

But sab kuch thoda ordinary lag raha tha.

Isliye maine decide kiya ki iss baar kuch different banaunga.  
Something jo sirf ek message na ho… balki ek feeling ho.

Ek small romantic website.  
Ek cinematic chat page.  
Jaise koi mini story slowly unfold ho rahi ho.

Usme:

- floating hearts honge
    
- soft romantic piano music hoga
    
- messages typing effect ke saath aayenge
    
- aur end mein ek line…
    

> “I’m really glad you exist.”

Pata nahi tujhe kitna pasand aayega.  
Pata nahi tu usse kaise feel karegi.  
But honestly… maine usme effort se zyada feelings daali hain.

Main usually itna open up nahi karta.  
But tere liye kuch create karte waqt realize hua ki tu mere liye kitni important ban chuki hai.

Har small detail pe overthink kar raha tha:

- colors kaise hone chahiye
    
- music kaunsa emotional lagega
    
- intro cinematic hona chahiye ya simple
    
- message ka timing kaisa ho
    

Because somewhere deep down…  
I wanted it to feel special.  
The way _you_ feel special to me.

Aur honestly…  
tera naam abhi bhi smile kara deta hai.

Shayad ye sab thoda cheesy lage future mein 😭  
But this is one of those rare moments jahan maine genuinely apna heart kisi cheez mein daala.

Agar kabhi future mein ye note dubara padhun…  
toh shayad mujhe yaad rahe:

> “There was a girl named Abhilasha…  
> jiske birthday ke liye maine sirf website nahi banayi thi,  
> maine ek feeling build ki thi.”

— Anand
