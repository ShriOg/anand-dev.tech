Kabhi kabhi kuch conversations normal nahi hote.  
Wo bas chats nahi rehte… memory ban jaate hain.  
Aur shayad ye unhi moments me se ek tha.

Us raat sab normal lag raha tha, but mujhe feel ho raha tha ki kuch toh hai jo wo chhupa rahi hai.  
Maine baar baar pucha:

> “Kuch batati kyu nhi ho mujheeee”  
> “Acha park me kya hua”

Aur har baar same reply:

> “Kuch nhi”  
> “Theek tha sb”

Par fir achanak usne ek line boli jo random nahi lagi.

> “Have i know you 20 seconds or 20 years”

Fir usne bola:

> “Always remember that line.”

Us moment pe mujhe exact meaning samajh nahi aaya tha.  
Bas laga ki wo indirectly kuch keh rahi hai.

Ab sochta hu toh shayad uska matlab tha:

Kuch log time se close nahi hote.  
Bas feel hote hain apne.

Chahe 20 second hue ho ya 20 saal, kuch connections explain nahi kiye ja sakte.  
Wo bas naturally exist karte hain.

Aur honestly…  
shayad isi liye wo line itni deeply hit kari.

Because somewhere inside, mujhe bhi lagta tha ki hamari bonding normal nahi thi.  
Comfort tha. Familiarity thi.  
Jaise pehle se jaante ho ek dusre ko.

Mujhe nahi pata future kya hai.  
Na ye pata ki wo line usne casually boli thi ya intentionally.

But haan —  
main us line ko yaad rakhunga.

> “Some people don’t become close in 20 years,  
> and some feel close in 20 seconds.”
