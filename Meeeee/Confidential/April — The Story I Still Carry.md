> *“Kabhi kabhi pyaar khatam nahi hota. Bas banda decide karta hai ki woh us pain me aur nahi reh sakta.”*

## Beginning

Main generally apni feelings kisi ko batata nahi hoon.  
Zyada tar cheezein andar hi rakhta hoon jab tak woh itni heavy na ho jaaye ki sambhalna mushkil ho.  
Yeh unhi cheezon me se ek hai.

Meri aur Abhilasha ki baat normal chal rahi thi. Hum ache se baat karte the.  
3 April ko maine usse jhumke diye the.

Woh din happy memory hona chahiye tha, but wahi se sab dheere dheere tootna shuru ho gaya.

---

## Mukesh, Hurt, Aur Woh Raat

Mukesh ne usse block kar diya kisi baat pe.  
Usse pehle bhi woh weird behave kar raha tha. Mere naam pe usse paise maang raha tha jabki maine kabhi usko bola bhi nahi tha.

Us raat Abhilasha bahut hurt thi.  
Woh show nahi kar rahi thi properly, but mujhe pata tha usko bura laga hai.

Shayad kisi ne usse “gold digger” type kuch bola tha.

Usne us raat apna frustration mujhpe nikala.  
Maine suna, kyunki mujhe pata tha woh hurt hai.

Next day uska tuition tha, phir woh ghoomne chali gayi, aur raat ko thak ke aayi toh zyada baat nahi hui.

---

## Overthinking Ka Start

Uske baad sab dheere dheere weird hone laga.

Ek raat maine dekha woh WhatsApp pe online thi aur Mukesh bhi online tha. Dono kaafi late tak online the.

Usne bola woh usse baat nahi kar rahi thi, but mera dimaag tab tak overthink karna shuru kar chuka tha.

### Feelings:
- Jealousy
- Insecurity
- Fear

Sab ek saath.

Maine message type kiya, phir delete kar diya.  
Laptop band kar diya.

Sach bolu toh main kehna chahta tha:

> “Please usse baat mat karo.”

But mujhe kabhi haq feel hi nahi hua uspe.  
Aur shayad pyaar haq ka naam bhi nahi hota.

Toh communicate karne ke instead main chup ho gaya.

Aur wahi meri sabse badi galti thi.

---

## Disappearing

Next day school gaya, institute gaya, but usse baat avoid karta raha.  
Jabki uss time usse meri zarurat thi.

Main apni hi anxiety me phas gaya tha.

Fir maine WhatsApp aur Instagram delete kar diya.

Mujhe laga:

> “Agar main yeh sab dekhna band kar dunga toh shayad anxiety bhi kam ho jayegi.”

Par maine ek baar bhi nahi socha ki usse kaisa feel hoga.

Maine apne decisions me usko involve hi nahi kiya.  
Bas khud se decide kar liya ki distance better hai.

Main 4 din ke liye gayab ho gaya.

Aur aaj bhi sochta hoon usse kitna bura laga hoga.

---

## Damage

Baad me jab maine usse message kiya toh obviously woh upset thi.

Maine bahut sorry bola, but kabhi kabhi “sorry” sabse useless word lagta hai.

Tab tak kuch badal chuka tha.

- Shayad meri absence ki wajah se.
- Shayad pehle se hi.
- Mujhe abhi tak nahi pata.

Par tab bhi mujhe lagta tha woh meri hai.  
Ya shayad main bas pagal tha.

Maine try kiya sab theek karne ka, but woh emotionally wapas nahi aayi.

Eventually usne mujhe block kar diya.

---

## Public Humiliation

Phir ek aur cheez hui.

Usne Preana naam ki ladki ko sab kuch bata diya. Personal cheezein. Woh chats somehow Mukesh tak pahunch gayi.

Fir institute me Mukesh sabke saamne baatein karne laga.

Cheap comments.  
Humiliation.

Main sirf head down karke baitha raha.

Mujhe pata tha agar maine upar dekha toh shayad ro padunga.

Mera mann kar raha tha usse maar du.  
But main chup raha.

Us hafte mentally main completely toot gaya tha.

Aur fir maine apni frustration ussi pe nikaal di.

Shayad wahi final damage tha.

## End

Uske baad sab khatam ho gaya.

Sabse zyada hurt yeh karta hai ki sabki life eventually normal ho gayi.

Mukesh aur uski bhi.

> “Bhai behen ki ladai temporary hoti hai.”

Bas hamara relation hi wapas normal nahi hua.

## Even Now

Aaj bhi main uske baare me sochta hoon.

Ek baar woh dikhi thi mujhe.  
Main uske side se jaldi nikal gaya kyunki himmat hi nahi hui rukne ki.

Main har jagah blocked hoon.  
Aur sach bolu toh dar bhi lagta hai.

Par woh aaj bhi utni hi pyari lagti hai.

Ek raat maine uski pictures almost 1.5 ghante tak dekhi thi continuously.

Uske birthday pe bhi wish nahi kiya.

Par fir bhi maine Mukesh ko bola tha uske liye kitchen set le lena kyunki mujhe laga usse pasand aayega.

## The Instagram Logins

Aur sabse ajeeb cheez?

Usne mere Instagram account me apne birthday pe **44 baar** login kiya.

Aur recently fir ek baar aur.

Shayad:
- curiosity thi,
- habit thi,
- ya memory.

Mujhe nahi pata.

Par mujhe itna pata hai:

> Jo bhi tha, real tha.

Perfect nahi tha.  
Mature nahi tha.  
Healthy bhi shayad nahi tha hamesha.

Par fake nahi tha.

## Her Last Message

Baad me usne mujhe ek long message bheja.

Woh angry message nahi tha.

Woh ek healing wala message tha.

Usne bola:
- woh move on kar rahi hai,
- strong ban rahi hai,
- overthinking se bahar aa rahi hai.

Usne mujhe thank you bhi bola ki maine usko samjhaya, support kiya.

Aur honestly…

woh message quiet way me bahut hurt karta hai.

Kyuki woh sach me peaceful lag rahi thi.

Aur shayad kabhi kabhi pyaar ka matlab yeh bhi hota hai —

> *“Ki tum kisi ki peace chaho, even agar us peace me tum khud na ho.”*

## Regret

Mujhe aaj bhi regret hai ki main communicate karne ke instead bhaag gaya.

Aur shayad meri life ka ek part hamesha yeh sochta rahega:

> “Agar main uss waqt chup hone ke instead baat kar leta… toh kya hota?”

## Why I Wrote This

Yeh note sympathy ke liye nahi hai.

Bas isliye hai kyunki main rarely kisi ko apni actual feelings batata hoon.

Aur yeh un few stories me se ek hai jahan maine kisi ko itna deeply pyaar kiya ki khud ko hi lose kar diya.
