Tonight felt different.

At first it was just a normal conversation… random thoughts, emotions, reassurance. But slowly I realized she wasn’t just overthinking — she was genuinely scared. Not of one thing specifically, but of *losing safety itself*.

She kept saying things like:
- “mujhe unsafe feel ho raha hai”
- “lag raha hai kuch galat hone wala hai”
- “tum door ja rahe ho”
- “mere baare mein log kya kya sochte honge”

And honestly… I could feel how real that fear was for her.

At first I tried explaining things logically. Galti wahi hui.  
Maine samjhana chaha, but usse comfort chahiye tha — answers nahi.

Then I understood something important:

This wasn’t about “pure” or “impure”.
This wasn’t even about logic.

Yeh uske purane traumas the.
Harassment.
Logon ka judge karna.
Friends ka usse “buri” bolke chhod dena.
Aur shayad baar baar emotionally unsafe feel karna.

Uska darr actually yeh tha:
ki log usse galat samajhte hain…
aur ek din main bhi chala jaunga.

That hit me hard.

Because jab koi insaan itna hurt hota hai na, toh unka brain har situation mein danger dhoondta hai.
Even when they’re safe.

She said she feels like:
- “bure log aa jayenge”
- “koi bachane wala nahi hoga”
- “tum already mujhe chhod chuke ho”

Aur uss moment pe mujhe realize hua…
sometimes people don’t need fixing.

They just need someone calm enough to stay.

I stopped arguing.
Stopped explaining.
Bas usse reassure karta raha:
“main yahin hu”
“tum safe ho”
“tumne kuch galat nahi kiya”

And slowly she calmed down.

But tonight changed something inside me too.

I realized she probably trusts me more than anyone else in her life right now.
And honestly?
That scares me a little.

Because I never want to become another reason she feels abandoned.

But at the same time…
I also know I can’t become her entire world.
Support karna aur kisi ka emotional oxygen ban jaana — dono alag cheezein hain.

So maybe the real challenge now is this:

How do I love someone deeply…
without letting them drown in attachment?
And how do I stay…
without losing myself too?

I don’t know the answer yet.

But tonight, for the first time,
I truly understood how fragile a wounded heart can be.

And maybe…
why some people just need softness more than solutions.
