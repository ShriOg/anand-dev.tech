## Context

Aaj usse dekha.

Bas kuch seconds ke liye.

Na baat hui.  
Na smile.  
Na koi dramatic moment.

Bas woh saamne se aa rahi thi.

Aur maine road cross kar li.

Us moment pe laga kuch nahi hua.

Par ghar pahochte hi chest heavy ho gayi.

Wahi purani feeling.

Jaise throat me kuch atak gaya ho.  
Jaise rona aa raha ho but aansu nahi aa rahe.  
Jaise dil ko kisi ne pakad rakha ho.

Aur tab mujhe laga ki shayad mujhe fir se likhna chahiye.

## Main Dar Kis Se Raha Tha?

Pehle mujhe lagta tha ki main usse dekhne se darta hu.

Aaj realize hua.

Main usse nahi darta.

Main uss version se darta hu jo usse dekhne ke baad mere andar zinda ho jata h.

Woh version jo:

- notifications ka wait karta tha
    
- late night 3 baje tak baat karta tha
    
- birthday website bana raha tha
    
- future imagine karta tha
    
- usse apna comfort zone maanta tha
    
- aur secretly believe karta tha ki shayad woh hamesha rahegi
    

Usse dekhte hi woh saari memories ek saath wapas aa jaati hain.

Aur shayad isi liye har cell bhaagna chahta tha.

## Meri Galti

Main ab isse deny nahi karta.

Bahut saare major moments me galti meri thi.

Main communicate kar sakta tha.

Par main chup ho gaya.

Main explain kar sakta tha.

Par main distance bana gaya.

Main ruk sakta tha.

Par main gayab ho gaya.

Aur relationship me silence kabhi kabhi argument se bhi zyada dangerous hoti hai.

Us waqt mujhe lag raha tha ki thoda space sab theek kar dega.

Par time neutral nahi hota.

Time cheezein change karta hai.

Jab main wapas aaya...

Shayad kuch already change ho chuka tha.

Aur shayad wahi part hai jo aaj bhi hurt karta hai.

Not because I lost her.

But because I don't know what would've happened if I had stayed.
