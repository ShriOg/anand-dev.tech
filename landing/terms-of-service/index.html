About MenuNova

MenuNova is a cloud-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform designed for restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, and food service businesses in India. Our Platform enables:

  • Digital Menu Management: Create and customize online menus with real-time updates
