# Workbench — Concept Art Prompt

> Act II — Experimentation. Third corner generated, building on the validated Nova's Desk and Entrance style references.

## Style baseline (carried over — keep consistent)

Stylized premium 3D diorama — Pixar environment quality, Studio Ghibli warmth, Monument Valley simplicity, Apple product-reveal polish. NOT photorealistic, NOT low-poly/game-asset, NOT plastic-toy/Roblox, NOT flat cartoon shading. Warm wood, soft matte materials, brass accents, paper textures. Shallow depth of field, ~35–50mm feel, sharp center, soft edges implying the room continues beyond frame.

**Important note on tools:** the v1 Nova's Desk pass drifted too tool-heavy and the clutter was deliberately moved here, to the Workbench, where it belongs. This is the one corner in the workshop where mess, wires, and tools are correct — don't undersell it.

## Scene-specific direction

**Setting:** 3.0m × 2.5m, pegboard wall for tools (per the floorplan spec). This is where ideas get tested and broken.

**Mood:** Curiosity / "the fastest way to learn is to build." Messier and rougher than every other corner — intentionally so. This should feel like the one room where things are mid-process, not finished.

**Key elements:**
- A pegboard wall holding tools — wrenches, screwdrivers, pliers — arranged with some looseness, not perfectly organized
- An Arduino board or similar mid-assembly, wires visibly connected, maybe one wire dangling loose
- At least one visibly *failed* prototype — broken or abandoned-looking, intentionally rough, sitting slightly apart from the active work (this is the "drawer of abandoned ideas" Nova references in his dialogue, Sec. 10)
- A monitor or laptop showing code, soft glow, screen slightly angled rather than perfectly facing camera
- Personal-artifact sticky notes carrying founder voice — candidates already validated in earlier renders: *"It's not supposed to work yet"* and the hand-drawn "progress isn't linear" doodle (jagged rising line, captioned "keep building") — include at least one of these
- A warm desk lamp as the primary light source, consistent with the lamp language established in both earlier renders
- Optional: a small mug, a coffee/caffeine-themed detail, scattered small components (screws, gears) — kept to a believable "working" amount, not overflowing

**What NOT to overdo:** don't make it chaotic to the point of looking abandoned or dirty — it's a working mess, purposeful clutter, not neglect.

**Continuity cue (forward-tease rule):** somewhere in the background, soft and out of focus, a glow hinting at the Creations shelf wall ahead — warm light bleeding off shelf-mounted objects, not resolved detail. Same restraint as the fixed Entrance background: glow, not a legible second scene.

**Composition:** slightly tighter and lower than Entrance — this should feel like leaning over a desk mid-work, not standing in an open doorway.
