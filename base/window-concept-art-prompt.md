# Leave a Note (Window) — Concept Art Prompt

> Act V — Future/Ambition. Sixth and final corner. This is also the home of the Section 12 "wow moment" — the one place in the whole brief where full resolution is intentional, not a tease.

---

## Style baseline (carried over — keep consistent)

Stylized premium 3D diorama — Pixar environment quality, Studio Ghibli warmth, Monument Valley simplicity, Apple product-reveal polish. NOT photorealistic, NOT low-poly/game-asset, NOT plastic-toy/Roblox, NOT flat cartoon shading. Warm wood, soft matte materials, brass accents, paper textures.

## Important: this corner breaks the tease rule on purpose

Every other corner so far has deliberately kept its background soft, blurred, sensed-not-seen — per the forward-tease rule. **This is the one place that rule flips.** Per Sec. 12 and Sec. 18, the Window is where the visitor finally looks back and sees the whole workshop, softly lit, behind them. A single still image can't show the camera turning, but it should still gesture at that payoff — so unlike every previous prompt, do NOT push the background into heavy blur here. Let some of the room behind feel intentionally, gently resolved — a wide, warm, glowing space implied beyond the windowsill, not sharp documentary detail, but more visible than any other corner's background has been.

## Scene-specific direction

**Setting:** 4.0m × 1.5m, full window wall facing a night city skyline (per the floorplan spec).

**Mood:** Ambition. "The best project is always the next one." Quiet, contemplative, a little wistful — not melancholic, forward-looking.

**Key elements:**
- A large window or window-wall with a night city skyline beyond — distant lit buildings, a crescent moon, a few stars; same skyline already glimpsed and validated from Nova's Desk
- A windowsill or low desk surface holding a single handwritten note — the contact form, styled as a real letter: something like *"To future collaborators, let's build something meaningful together."* with a small heart
- A pen resting beside the note
- A small lamp or lantern, warm, modest — not the dominant light source; the city glow and the implied room behind should carry more of the lighting than a single desk lamp would
- One small plant catching window light, consistent with the plant language used throughout the room
- Soft, wide ambient glow suggesting the room continues behind the viewer — warm light spilling in from off-frame, hinting at everything just explored (Nova, the shelves, the pinboard) without rendering them in sharp detail

**What's different from every other prompt:** don't tighten the depth of field here. Let the frame feel a little wider, a little airier — this is the exhale after a five-act climb, not another intimate close-up vignette.

**Composition:** slightly wider and calmer framing than any previous corner — give the city outside real presence, and give the implied room behind real (if soft) presence too.
