## Prompt v2 (revised after first-pass review)

**What changed from v1:** the first pass drifted toward "Workbench" territory — too many loose tools, gears, and a pegboard of wrenches/screwdrivers made the scene read as a workshop bench rather than a calmer personal desk. v2 pulls back the clutter and tightens the background so it teases the *next* zone rather than just "more workshop."

A single cinematic interior scene: a cozy handcrafted desk corner at night, centered on a small robot companion sitting on the desk surface. Stylized premium 3D diorama style — Pixar environment quality, Studio Ghibli warmth, Monument Valley simplicity, Apple product-reveal polish. NOT photorealistic, NOT low-poly/game-asset, NOT plastic-toy/Roblox, NOT flat cartoon shading. "Stylized reality" — slightly exaggerated warmth, believable light and material behavior.

**The robot companion (Nova):** small, round, cute but not childish — soft matte-white shell, expressive face panel with simple glowing cyan eyes (closed-smile crescent shape), rounded limbs, slight forward lean as if listening. Reads as a resident/companion, not a gadget. Faint warm glow from his face panel lights the desk surface near him.

**The desk — calmer than v1:** warm-toned wood, lived-in but tidy. Keep: a softly glowing tablet/monitor with soft-focus UI glow, a mug, a small brass desk lamp casting warm directional light, one notebook with a few loose pages. **Remove or drastically reduce:** scattered screwdrivers, gears, circuit boards, loose screws — at most one small subtle tool resting quietly, not a cluttered workbench spread. This is a desk for thinking and talking, not actively repairing something mid-task.

**Background — fix the continuity cue:** instead of a second distinct desk with its own tool pegboard (which currently reads as an unrelated second room), soften the background into an implied transition: dim, unfocused shapes that *suggest* the Workbench zone is nearby without fully resolving it — e.g. a faint glow of tools just out of crisp focus, deep in shadow, more like a peripheral glimpse than a second composed scene. The goal: "I can sense the next room," not "here is another room."

**Lighting:** same as v1 — warm lamp + Nova's cool cyan glow as the two dominant light sources, restrained two-temperature palette, soft falloff into shadow at the frame edges.

**Materials:** warm wood grain, soft matte plastics/ceramics, a little paper texture on the notebook, brass on the lamp. Avoid shiny plastic, glassmorphism, neon/cyberpunk surfaces, hard sci-fi metal.

**Mood/atmosphere:** quiet, intimate, late-night conversation feeling — calm, not "mid-repair." Emotional target: connection.

**Composition:** medium-close, desk-height eye-line, Nova roughly centered or slightly off-center, soft falloff at the edges implying the room continues beyond frame.

**Camera/lens feel:** shallow depth of field, ~35–50mm at a wide-ish aperture, sharp on Nova and the immediate desk surface, soft at the edges — background especially soft, to support the "implied, not resolved" continuity cue above.

## Original v1 prompt (for reference)

A single cinematic interior scene: a cozy handcrafted workshop corner at night, centered on a small robot companion sitting on a wooden desk. Stylized premium 3D diorama style — think Pixar environment quality, Studio Ghibli warmth, Monument Valley simplicity, Apple product-reveal polish. NOT photorealistic, NOT low-poly/game-asset style, NOT plastic-toy/Roblox aesthetic, NOT flat cartoon shading. "Stylized reality" — slightly exaggerated proportions and warmth, but materials and light behave believably.

**The robot companion (Nova):** small, round, cute but not childish — soft matte-white shell, a single expressive face panel with simple glowing cyan eyes (closed-smile crescent shape, not realistic pupils), rounded limbs, sits with a slight forward lean as if listening. He should read as a companion/resident, not a gadget or toy. Warm soft glow emanates faintly from his face panel, lighting the desk surface near him.

**The desk:** warm-toned wood, slightly worn/lived-in (not pristine), holding: a softly glowing monitor or tablet screen (subtle UI glow, frosted/soft-focus, not sharp interface detail), a mug, a small reading lamp casting warm directional light, a notebook with loose pages, a few scattered small tools/parts. Everything slightly cluttered but intentional — lived-in, not messy.

**Lighting:** warm, low-key, atmospheric — one or two practical light sources (the lamp, the screen glow, Nova's own glow) doing most of the work, soft falloff into shadow at the edges of frame. Overall palette: warm amber/wood tones grounding the scene, with one cool accent (Nova's cyan glow, or a hint of blue screen-light) for contrast — keep the palette restrained, two dominant temperature families only.

**Materials:** warm wood grain, soft matte plastics/ceramics (not glossy/plastic-toy), a little frosted glass or soft fabric texture nearby, paper textures on the notebook. Avoid: shiny plastic, glassmorphism panels, neon/cyberpunk surfaces, hard sci-fi metal.

**Mood/atmosphere:** quiet, intimate, late-night-tinkering feeling — calm rather than dramatic, inviting rather than spectacular. The emotional target is "connection" — this is the one corner in the workshop where the visitor feels closest to a person, not a portfolio.

**Composition:** medium-close, desk-height eye-line, Nova roughly centered or slightly off-center, enough negative space/soft falloff around the edges to suggest the rest of a larger room continues beyond frame (this corner is part of one continuous space, not an isolated diorama).

**Camera/lens feel:** slightly shallow depth of field, like a 35–50mm lens at a wide-ish aperture — sharp focus on Nova and the desk surface immediately around him, gentle softness at the edges.
