# Mindset — Concept Art Prompt

> Act IV — Reflection/Systems. Fifth corner generated. Deliberately different prop language from Creations — see note below.

---

## Style baseline (carried over — keep consistent)

Stylized premium 3D diorama — Pixar environment quality, Studio Ghibli warmth, Monument Valley simplicity, Apple product-reveal polish. NOT photorealistic, NOT low-poly/game-asset, NOT plastic-toy/Roblox, NOT flat cartoon shading. Warm wood, soft matte materials, brass accents, paper textures. Shallow depth of field, sharp center, soft edges.

## Critical distinction from Creations — read this first

Creations was "things I've built" — museum energy, individually spotlit trophies, finished artifacts in display niches. **Mindset must feel categorically different: "how I think."** If this corner ends up looking like another shelf of objects on display, it has failed its one job. The visual language here is pinned paper, connection, and process — not display cases.

## Scene-specific direction

**Setting:** 3.5m × 2.0m — taller than deep, more vertical wall space than floor objects (per the floorplan spec).

**Mood:** Reflection. Calmer and more spacious than every other corner so far — fewer objects, more breathing room, deliberately slower pacing.

**Key elements:**
- A pinboard or wall area covered in pinned papers — handwritten principle cards connected by string or thread into a visible sequence, e.g. something like: *Focus on Impact → Build for Humans → Ship Early → Learn Fast → Iterate*
- One or two framed sketches representing mental models or systems diagrams (simple boxes-and-arrows style, hand-drawn feeling, not polished infographics)
- A small writing surface or desk beneath the pinboard — a single open notebook, a pen, maybe one warm small lamp, kept minimal
- At most one or two books as minor background texture only — not the focal object, not arranged as a "shelf of books" the way Creations was
- Soft personal-artifact sticky notes consistent with the founder voice already established (e.g. "Systems Scale Better," "Focus on Impact" — already validated as concepts in earlier mockup references)

**What NOT to include:** rows of book spines as the primary visual, a literal "library" feel, anything that reads as a display case or trophy shelf — that vocabulary belongs to Creations, not here.

**Lighting:** softer and more even than the other corners' single dramatic spotlight — diffuse, contemplative light, supporting the calmer "Reflection" pacing rather than a single dramatic hero-light.

**Continuity cue (forward-tease rule):** a small, soft, out-of-focus warm glow in the background hinting at Nova's desk nearby — his presence sensed (a faint cyan-tinted warmth, not a resolved figure), consistent with the restraint validated at every corner so far.

**Composition:** more vertical framing than previous corners, since the pinboard wall is the visual subject rather than a horizontal row of objects — let it breathe, with generous negative space.
