## Style baseline (carried over — keep consistent)

Stylized premium 3D diorama — Pixar environment quality, Studio Ghibli warmth, Monument Valley simplicity, Apple product-reveal polish. NOT photorealistic, NOT low-poly/game-asset, NOT plastic-toy/Roblox, NOT flat cartoon shading. Warm wood, soft matte materials, brass accents, paper textures. Shallow depth of field, sharp center, soft edges.

## Scene-specific direction

**Setting:** 3.0m × 2.5m, lit shelf wall holding the three project dioramas (per the floorplan spec).

**Mood:** Pride. "Ideas deserve to exist in the real world." Each project should feel like a trophy earned through iteration — displayed with care, individually lit, not crammed together.

**Key elements — three project dioramas, each distinct and individually spotlit:**
1. **MenuNova** — the flagship. A small phone or tablet mockup showing a restaurant-ordering interface, warmly lit, maybe a tiny accompanying prop (a miniature menu card, a small plate icon) to ground it as "restaurant tech" without needing text to explain it
2. **Nova AI Companion** — a glowing screen or small standalone display with a soft chat-bubble motif, paired with a small Nova-style figure or icon nearby to visually tie it back to the character living elsewhere in the room
3. **Smart Waste Management** — a small finished rover/robot model, more polished and complete-looking than its rougher, mid-build counterpart implied to exist back at the Workbench

**Important continuity detail:** leave a visible fourth slot on the shelf — empty, but lit as if reserved, with no object in it. This is a deliberate, quiet hint that "the next project hasn't been built yet," seeding the Window's closing line (Sec. 12) without stating anything outright at this stage.

**Personal-artifact candidates:** a small "Shipped. Learned. Iterated." or similar founder-voice note tucked near the shelf (style consistent with the sticky notes validated at Workbench and Entrance) — optional but on-brand if it fits naturally.

**Lighting:** each project diorama gets its own small warm spotlight/accent, creating a row of individually lit "exhibits" rather than one flat wash of light across the whole shelf — reinforces "trophy earned through iteration" rather than a generic display case.

**Continuity cue (forward-tease rule):** a soft, out-of-focus glow in the background hinting at Mindset's bookshelf lamp ahead — same restrained treatment validated at Entrance and Workbench: sensed warmth, not a resolved second scene.

**Composition:** wide enough to read all three (plus the empty fourth slot) as a row, but framed so no single project visually dominates over MenuNova — it should still feel like the flagship even while sharing the shelf.
